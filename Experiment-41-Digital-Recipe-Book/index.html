Authentic Tuscan Wild Mushroom Risotto

⏱️ Prep: 15 mins 🔥 Cook: 30 mins ⭐ 4.95 (380 reviews) 🌱 Vegetarian Friendly
Adjust Servings: 4

Ingredients Checklist

300g Arborio Carnaroli Rice
250g Fresh Porcini & Cremini Mushrooms
1000ml Simmering Vegetable Stock
120ml Dry White Italian Wine
60g Grated Parmigiano-Reggiano
40g Unsalted Cold Butter

Step-by-Step Cooking Guide

1
Sauté the Mushrooms: Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a heavy-bottomed skillet over medium-high heat. Sauté sliced mushrooms until deeply golden. Reserve half for garnish.
2
Toast the Rice: In a wide saucepan, sweat finely chopped shallots in butter. Add the Arborio rice and toast for 2 minutes until translucent around the edges.
3
Deglaze & Simmer: Pour in the dry white wine, stirring continuously until absorbed. Begin ladling hot vegetable stock one scoop at a time, allowing each ladle to absorb before adding the next.
4
Mantecatura Finish: Remove from heat. Vigorously beat in cold cubed butter and freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano to create a velvety emulsion. Serve immediately with cracked pepper.
